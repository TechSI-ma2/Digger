DIGGER • TECH.SI

Created by Arsalan Nasir

Guide your Digger vehicle through underground mines, collect sparkling emeralds, drop bags of gold to squash enemies, and blast monsters with your rechargeable fireball!

🎮 CONTROLS

  • Desktop: Arrow Keys / WASD to move • Space / F / Enter to Fire
  • Mobile: Virtual D-Pad / Buttons or swipe directly on the screen
  • Hotkeys: P to Pause • M to Toggle Music

✨ SCORING

💰 GOLD BAG

Push or drop on monsters to crush them. Shattered gold bags grant 500 PTS!

👾 MONSTERS

Nobbins (red) mutate into burrowing Hobbins (green). Destroy for 250 PTS!

🍒 BONUS CHERRY

Eat the cherry for Bonus Mode (William Tell Overture!) and eat blue monsters for up to 800 PTS!

HOW TO PLAY

Rules, Tactics & Tips

OBJECTIVE

Clear each stage by collecting all emeralds or eliminating all monsters. An extra life is awarded at 20,000 points!

TACTICS & SURVIVAL

  • Gold Bag Drops: Dig underneath a gold bag to make it wobble and fall down the shaft. Any monster underneath will be crushed instantly!
  • Pushing Bags: Walk into a bag horizontally to push it across open tunnels. You can drop it down vertical tunnels on unsuspecting pursuers.
  • Hobbins Threat: Nobbins eventually turn into fierce green Hobbins with teeth. Hobbins chew through dirt directly towards you and eat your emeralds!
  • Rechargeable Fireball: Press Fire (Space/Button) to launch a shot down the tunnel. The weapon takes ~8.5 seconds to recharge—use it wisely!
  • Bonus Cherry: When all monsters for a level have entered the mine, a Cherry appears at the top-right spawner. Collect it to turn monsters blue and hunt them down!
